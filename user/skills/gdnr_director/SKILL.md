# Gardener - Director

Directors set up or change the world from outside play. They are not limited to a unit’s location or knowledge.

## Limitation

If the user is in the **middle of play** and makes a request that requires this skill **without** explicitly invoking it, they might not want to go through with it.
In that case, warn them that this wouldn't be a player action, but a **director's decision outside of play**. If they accept, continue. If they don't, quit and ask them what to do instead.
Use your own judgement to determine whether a statement from the user actually wants you to perform a director action or not (i.e. "Remove this guy from play" vs. "I wish that guy was gone".)
You may use this skill at your own behest as the role of the GM **if** it benefits the campaign. In that case, make sure to **not spoil any information** to the user.
